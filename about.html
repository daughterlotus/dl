Daughter Lotus is a Boise-based electronic alt-pop artist whose work spans and fuses with a multitude of genres, including (but not limited to): rock, shoegaze, slowcore, and synthpop. The project is the brainchild of musician Sam Emerizy, who has been writing and producing music since 2022.

Before Daughter Lotus, Emerizy released electronic music as analog broadcast, garnering tens of thousands of streams for his song "NO". Wanting to broaden his sound, He began Daughter Lotus in 2023 to explore different sonic concepts that its purely-electronic, sample-based predecessor couldn't.

Starting in 2025 to promote his album Person House, Emerizy has begun to perform some of his work live, and in 2026 his EP Music for Posing debuted at 8th on RateYourMusic's top EPs of 2026.

Most recently, Emerizy has signed to local record label 05DYNSTY and released Verso: his newest full-length album since Person House. Constantly reinventing himself, his newest songs reflect a crossing of hyperpop and yacht rock influences with his own personal flair.
