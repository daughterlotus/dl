Daughter Lotus is a Boise-based electronic alt-pop artist whose work spans and fuses with a multitude of genres, including (but not limited to): rock, shoegaze, slowcore, and synthpop. The project is the brainchild of musician Sam Emerizy, who has been writing and producing music since 2022.

Before Daughter Lotus, Emerizy released electronic music as analog broadcast, at one point garnering over twenty-thousand streams for his song "NO". Wanting to broaden his sound, He began Daughter Lotus in 2023 to explore different soundscapes that its purely-electronic, sample-based predecessor couldn't.

His most recent EP, Music for Posing debuted at 8th on community platform RateYourMusic's top EPs of 2026.



Starting in 2025 to promote his latest album release Person House, Emerizy has begun to perform some of his work live at the Boise-local venue Realms Arcade.
